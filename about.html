Our story

A simple home
in the hills.

We started Sunrise Villa because we love this corner of Lebong — the quiet mornings, the mountain light, and the way Darjeeling feels like home once you slow down.

Our rooms are clean, comfortable and uncomplicated. We are at Golai Bazar, close enough to explore Darjeeling but far enough from the noise to sleep well.

When you stay with us, you are not checking into a hotel chain. You are staying with a family that lives here, knows the area, and is happy to help you plan your days.
