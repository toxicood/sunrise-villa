Common questions

Good to know.

If you need anything else, just call or WhatsApp us — we reply quickly.

How do I book a room?

Use the booking form on this website or send us your dates on WhatsApp. We confirm availability the same day.

Do I need to pay in advance?

For most dates, no advance payment is needed. You can pay on arrival. For peak season we may ask for a small advance.

Is food available?

Tea and simple home-style meals can be arranged on request. There are also good local eateries nearby.

Can you arrange a taxi or sightseeing?

Yes, we can help you arrange local taxis and suggest a plan for Darjeeling, tea gardens and nearby viewpoints.
